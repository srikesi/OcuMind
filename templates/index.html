Calibration Guide

Before we begin the exercise, we need to map the tracker to your eyes. This takes about 30 seconds.

Step 1: Focus (Yellow)

A Yellow dot will appear. Move your eyes to focus on it immediately.

You will hear 3 short low-pitched beeps as a countdown.

Step 2: Hold (Blue)

The dot will turn Blue. Hold your gaze perfectly still until it disappears.

You will hear a steady high-pitched tone while data is being captured.

Step 3: Success (Green)

Points you have completed successfully will flash Green.

Final: The Exercise (Pink)

Once all 9 points are done, the Pink dot will appear and start moving.

Ignore all other dots and follow only the pink target!

A rising chime will signal the start of the session.
